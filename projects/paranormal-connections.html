PART-1: FOUR CORNERS GAME

Four friends discover a pendrive containing paranormal documents. When they follow the rules of a four-corners game, an evil entity takes one of them into its own dimension. Veer discovers a way to enter that world and rescue his friend—but becomes trapped himself, until his brother’s soul finds him and sends him back.

CREATIVE CHALLENGE 01

Making the idea real.

The story needed a believable paranormal-game PDF, so I researched spirit-calling games and wrote or modified the rules and documents to fit the plot.

1 / 3
CREATIVE CHALLENGE 02

Building the atmosphere.

The film used red light for the evil presence and blue light for the brother’s soul. With a limited budget, we improvised coloured light using laptops, tablets, phone torches, tape and marker.

05 / THE RESULT

What worked. What I learned.

Feedback highlighted the importance of camera quality, clear dialogue, well-matched music and choosing the right runtime for a short film.

LESSON 01

Prepare the craft

Plan image and sound quality from the beginning and collaborate with the right creative team.

LESSON 02

Protect the sound

Record clean dialogue or plan for dubbing, and listen critically throughout production.

LESSON 03

Review the whole scene

Judge continuity, pacing and transitions across connected clips, not only isolated shots.
